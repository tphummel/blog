Amtrak's California Zephyr runs daily from Chicago to Emeryville, across the bay from San Francisco. It is about 2,438 miles and a little under 53 hours, with two nights aboard.

{{< figure src="/img/california-zephyr/map.svg" title="Chicago to Emeryville on the California Zephyr" alt="Map of the California Zephyr route from Chicago, Illinois across Iowa, Nebraska, Colorado, Utah and Nevada to Emeryville, California, with the top stops marked" >}}

## Top stops

- **Chicago, IL** &mdash; the start, from Union Station.
- **Omaha, NE** &mdash; the Missouri River crossing and the end of the Midwest farmland.
- **Denver, CO** &mdash; the halfway point and the first morning, with the Rockies ahead.
- **Moffat Tunnel, CO** &mdash; the climb west of Denver, topping out around 9,200 ft before a 6-mile tunnel under the Continental Divide.
- **Glenwood Springs, CO** &mdash; after the train follows the Colorado River through Glenwood Canyon.
- **Grand Junction, CO** &mdash; the gateway to Ruby Canyon and the Utah desert.
- **Salt Lake City, UT** &mdash; across the Great Salt Lake desert and into Nevada.
- **Reno, NV** &mdash; the last stop before the Sierra Nevada.
- **Truckee, CA** &mdash; up and over Donner Pass.
- **Sacramento, CA** &mdash; down out of the mountains and into the Central Valley.
- **Emeryville, CA** &mdash; the finish. An Amtrak Thruway bus continues into San Francisco.

## Family bedroom pricing

The family bedroom is the Superliner's private room for four: two adults and two children, on the lower level of the sleeping car. There is only one per sleeper car, so they sell out.

Amtrak sells sleeping rooms as a package with the rail fare for everyone in the room, and the price climbs as the train fills.

| Trip | Typical price for the room |
|---|---|
| Chicago to Emeryville, one way | about $2,200&ndash;$2,500 (a 2026 route guide estimate) |
| Chicago to Emeryville, older fares | about $1,200 at the lowest fare bucket, about $2,100 at the highest |
| Denver to San Francisco, older fare | about $1,300 |

For comparison, a standard two-person bedroom on the full route was reported at about $2,200 in 2026. A roomette for two on the shorter Denver to San Francisco run was about $520.

These are ballpark numbers, not quotes. Fares depend on the date and how early you book, so check [amtrak.com](https://www.amtrak.com) for the actual price.
