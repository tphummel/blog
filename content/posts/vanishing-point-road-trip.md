Denver to San Francisco, through Cisco, Utah and over Sonora Pass into the Sierra Nevada.

{{< figure src="/img/vanishing-point-road-trip/map.svg" title="Denver to San Francisco via Cisco, UT and Sonora Pass" alt="Map of a road trip route from Denver, Colorado through Cisco, Utah, across Nevada, and over Sonora Pass in California to San Francisco" >}}

## The route

- **Denver, CO** &rarr; **Cisco, UT** &mdash; I-70 west through the Rockies and across the Utah desert.
- **Cisco, UT** &rarr; **Sonora Pass, CA** &mdash; cutting across Nevada on US-50, the "Loneliest Road in America."
- **Sonora Pass, CA** &rarr; **San Francisco, CA** &mdash; over CA-108 and down through the Central Valley.

Total trip: about 20 hours of driving.

This is the intended route from the [Vanishing Point](https://www.themoviedb.org/movie/11951-vanishing-point) movie.

White Dodge Challenger optional.
