Annual Journal Layout

Pages (left–right)SpreadDatesWeek of yearLife weekLife daysYear doneSquares (of 48)Month grid

The month grid shows the month the week is filed under, with the week's days shaded (including any days that spill into the neighboring month). Each spread is two facing pages: even page on the left, odd page on the right. A monthly spread opens each month. Weeks run Monday–Sunday. A week that straddles two months stays before the next monthly spread if at least 3 of its days fall in the ending month; with only 1–2 days in the ending month it goes after the next monthly spread. The week containing January 1 belongs to the prior year's journal (its final weekly spread), so the year starts with the January monthly and then the first Monday-start week. Week of year counts Monday-start weeks, with week 1 being the first full week of January (the first Monday on or after January 1). Years before 2027 keep the old rule, where week 1 is the week containing January 1. Life days run Monday through Sunday and are counted so the birthdate is life day 2; the life week is the one containing the week's Sunday. Year done is the share of the year complete through the week's Sunday (capped at 100%), and squares is that share of a 6×8 grid (48 squares), where a partial square is fine. The week containing December 31 is this year's final weekly spread.
