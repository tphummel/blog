Annual Journal Layout

PagesSpreadDates

Each spread is two pages. A monthly spread opens each month. Weeks run Monday–Sunday. A week with 3+ days in the next month follows that month's monthly spread; a week with 1–2 days of spill-over comes before it. The week containing January 1 belongs to the prior year's journal (its final weekly spread), so the year starts with the January monthly and then the first Monday-start week. The week containing December 31 is this year's final weekly spread.
